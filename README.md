# OIRAM 🐔

Welcome to OIRAM!

Here, I will teach you everything you need to know!

Gameplay!:
1. Movement: Use A and D to move left and right and W/Space to jump!
2. Enemy Stomping: Jump/fall onto an enemy to squish them!
3. Collectables: Collect Burburs and coins! Coins are for buying new skins, and Burburs are for turning into coins!


Features!:
1. OST: 4 great songs to listen to with stuff like: Cluckin' Through the Grass, Too Hot to Cluck, Burburs Below Zero, And A Burbur Beginning! There are also 3 jingles, THE FINAL CLUCK: Game Victory, Cluck Around and Find Out: Death, And Flagged for Cluckcess: Level Complete!
2. 12 epic skins to choose from!
3. 3 worlds to explore

Thank you for reading this!

Live long, and God bless!
